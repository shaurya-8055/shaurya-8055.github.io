What it does

🔌 Offline-first

Reminders create, edit and fire with no connection, then reconcile against Firestore the moment one returns.

🗣️ Say it in plain English

"Remind me to call mom every Sunday evening" becomes a real schedule. Voice input and text-to-speech built in.

⏰ Alarms that actually fire

AlarmManager for exact on-device timing that survives Doze and reboots; FCM for updates from teammates.

👥 Shared reminders

Team reminders with role-based access, so a group of five stays on one schedule.

☁️ Serverless backend

NestJS on AWS Lambda, 41 endpoints, with backup and export.

💳 Subscriptions

RevenueCat billing with tiered entitlements.
