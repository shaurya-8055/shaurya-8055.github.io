SnapFloat

Screenshots that float, then disappear. A thumbnail floats in the corner; drag it into any app. Nothing piles up in your Screenshots folder unless you save it.

  • Markup with one-click private-info blur, OCR text copy, QR reading, scrolling capture that stitches long pages, and screen recording.
  • Runs on GNOME Wayland through a small Shell extension, and on Windows with a real installer.
  • Every release is self-tested on CI before it's published: the AppImage, the exe and the installer.

SynClock

Family reminders with accountability. Type or say a line and it becomes an exact alarm: an on-device parser handles everyday phrasing in microseconds, with Gemini as a cached fallback.

  • Offline-first Flutter client with exact alarms, a live home-screen widget and reminders read aloud in Hindi and English.
  • Family and work teams with notification rules, snooze escalation and nudges, on a NestJS API deployed by GitHub Actions.
  • Rotating refresh tokens with replay detection and about 320 automated tests.
